## English (primary)

**Subtitle:** Recipes, meal plans & pantry
**Alternative subtitle:** Your private recipe box

**Promotional text:**
Save any recipe from TikTok, YouTube or the web in seconds — on-device AI turns shared posts into structured recipes. Private by design.

**Keywords:**
recipe,recipes,meal,planner,cooking,grocery,shopping,list,pantry,import,tiktok,ai,food,nutrition

**Description:**

Your recipes, from anywhere — organized, planned, and on your shopping list.

SAVE RECIPES FROM ANYWHERE
See a recipe on TikTok, YouTube or a website? Share it to Koen's Kitchen and on-device AI turns it into a structured recipe: ingredients, amounts, and steps. Snap a photo of a cookbook page, paste text, or just type a dish name and let AI draft the recipe for you.

ORGANIZED YOUR WAY
Categories, color-coded tags, favorites, ratings, photos, and powerful filters — including "What can I cook?", which shows only recipes you can make with what's in your pantry right now.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
Drop recipes on the meal calendar, set portions, and see daily and weekly nutrition summaries powered by the NEVO food database.

SHOP SMARTER
Generate a shopping list from your meal plan with one tap. Items are grouped by supermarket aisle — in the order you choose — and quantities already in your pantry are deducted automatically.

KNOW YOUR PANTRY
Track what's in your fridge, freezer, and cupboards. Scan barcodes to add products, get reminders before food expires, and waste less.

COOK HANDS-FREE
Cook Mode guides you step by step and keeps the screen awake while you cook.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, no cloud servers. AI processing runs entirely on-device with Apple Intelligence. Export your data anytime as JSON.

In your language: English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Español, Polski.

Note: AI-powered import requires Apple Intelligence (iOS 26, supported devices). All other features work on every device.
